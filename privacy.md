# Privacy Policy for QuizLock

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026  
**Developer:** Kelly Ideas  
**Contact:** [kellyideas.dev@gmail.com](mailto:kellyideas.dev@gmail.com)

QuizLock (`io.github.kellyideas.quizlock`) is an offline cognitive quiz and screen-time budget tracker developed by **Kelly Ideas**.

## Data Collection and Sharing

QuizLock does **not** collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal or device data.

- **No Internet Access:** The Google Play edition of QuizLock does not request the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission and cannot make network connections.
- **Local-Only Storage:** All quiz attempts, earned minute balances, streaks, and user settings (such as theme and language preferences) are stored strictly locally on your device.
- **Family Page (Direct edition only, off by default):** The Direct edition (`io.github.kellyideas.quizlock.unrestricted`, not distributed through Google Play) has an optional family page. Only when you turn it on, the app sends a small progress snapshot to the pack server address that you set, which is your own server on your home network. The snapshot holds a random device ID made by the app, the child or phone name, and progress numbers: balance, savings jar, streak, minutes earned and used today, answers and right answers of the last 7 days, chores waiting and the number of alerts. It is sent at most every 5 minutes, and only when the balance changed. Nothing is sent to Kelly Ideas or any third party. Plain HTTP is used only for private network addresses. The server keeps the snapshots in memory only. Turn the switch off to stop it.
- **Not Included in Backups:** The app database is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to your Google account or to a new phone. The same applies to your permission choices, the app's on-device usage log, today's snooze count, the lock timers, the internal lock token, the tamper alerts, pending Commitment mode changes and the family page settings.
- **On-Device Usage Log:** QuizLock keeps a short log of app events (for example, which permissions you allowed or declined and from which screen) to improve the app. It holds no personal data, keeps only the newest 500 entries, and never leaves your device.
- **Data Deletion:** You can delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising:** QuizLock contains no advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or third-party tracking scripts.
