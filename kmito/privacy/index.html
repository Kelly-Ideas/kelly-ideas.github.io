Privacy Policy for Kmito

Effective Date: October 24, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 14, 2026  ·  Developer: Kelly Ideas  ·  Package ID: io.github.kellyideas.kmito

Kmito is a cognitive quiz and screen-time budget application developed by Kelly Ideas. It works fully offline.

1. Data Collection and Sharing

Kmito does not collect or share personal data. The only data that can leave your phone is anonymous usage data, and only if you turn it on (see Anonymous usage data below). Kmito never sells data and shows no ads.

  • Internet Access: Kmito uses the internet only to send anonymous usage data after you opt in. The Google Play release downloads no updates or content from the internet.
  • 100% On-Device Storage: All quiz answers, earned screen-time minute balances, daily streaks, and application settings (such as theme and language preferences) are stored strictly locally in your device's internal application database.
  • Microphone (Optional, for Voice Answers): Kmito asks for microphone access only if you want to answer quizzes by voice. You can decline and answer by tapping instead. When you use voice answers, Kmito passes your speech to Android's speech recognition, gets back text, and compares that text with the answer choices. Kmito does not record, save or upload audio, and it keeps no transcripts. On Android 12 and later, when your phone has on-device speech recognition, Kmito uses it and the audio stays on your phone. Otherwise Android uses your phone's default speech recognition service (often Google's), which may process the audio on its own servers under that provider's privacy policy. Kelly Ideas receives none of this audio or text.
  • Not Included in Backups: The app database is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to your Google account or to a new phone.
  • Anonymous Usage Data (Optional, Off by Default): In Settings → About you can turn on Share anonymous usage data. Only then does Kmito send app events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service run by Google for Kelly Ideas. The events say which screens and steps were used, for example which permission was allowed or declined, whether setup was finished, and whether the quiz lock is on. They never contain your name, email, quiz answers, the names of your apps, or what you do in other apps. Firebase also receives technical data that comes with every event: a random app-install ID made by Firebase, device model, Android version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from the network address. Kmito does not use the advertising ID or the Android ID, sets no user ID, and turns off ad personalization. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and Google Analytics keeps it for 2 months. Turn the switch off at any time: sending stops at once and the app-install ID is deleted from your phone. While Child Guard is on, nothing is sent. See policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • No Advertising: Kmito contains no advertising SDKs and no crash-reporting SDKs. The only third-party service is the optional Google Analytics for Firebase above.

2. Data Retention and Deletion

Your quiz data and settings live only on your device, so you have full control over them. You can reset your data inside the app's Settings screen, clear the application storage in your Android system settings, or uninstall the app to permanently remove all locally stored records. Anonymous usage data already sent holds no name or email, so it cannot be traced back to you; Google Analytics deletes it automatically after 2 months.
