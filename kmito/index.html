Packs

You pick what it asks.

Turn packs on and off. The question on the card comes only from the packs that are on.

Each pack answers in its own way: a tap, a typed number, a marker on a line, a word for the gap.

Packs for your children too.

Eleven packs are written for younger readers: nine for ages 7–9 and two for ages 10–13. You turn them on like any other pack. Pick one to see how it asks.

Seven moments

A moment in a day, not a feature.

  1. 01

    The reflex reach

    Before the feed: one question first.

    Balance for the whole phone. The lock comes only at zero, after warnings at 5 min and at 1 min.

  2. 02

    The exam in four months

    Every unlock is one exam question.

    English B2 pack with explanations in Ukrainian. Missed questions come back in the review deck.

  3. 03

    The night scroll

    After 23:00, the phone asks first. Calls still come through.

    Schedules for a night window. The lock steps aside for calls and alarms, and no minutes drain meanwhile.

  4. 04

    The puzzle habit

    Your daily tactics, at the door of your phone.

    Chess on a real board, speed math on a keypad, logic, physics, algorithms. A skill rating per category.

  5. 05

    The careful starter

    Start with your usual day. Lose a little each week.

    Easy start: minutes on the house that shrink week by week, a soft lock first, each step announced a day ahead.

  6. 06

    The busy phone

    Maps, bank and work chat stay free. Only the feed costs minutes.

    Kmito reads the last week of use on the phone and suggests always-free apps and daily limits.

  7. 07

    The promise kept

    Tightening is instant. Loosening waits 24 h.

    Commitment mode delays every change that weakens the lock. A partner PIN puts the key in a friend's hands.
