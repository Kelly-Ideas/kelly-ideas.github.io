# Privacy Policy for Kmito

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 17, 2026  
**Developer:** Kelly Ideas  
**Contact:** [kellyideas.team@gmail.com](mailto:kellyideas.team@gmail.com)

Kmito (`io.github.kellyideas.kmito`) is a cognitive quiz and screen-time budget tracker developed by **Kelly Ideas**. It works fully offline.

## Data Collection and Sharing

Kmito does **not** collect or share personal data. The only data that can leave your phone is anonymous usage data, and only if you turn it on (see *Anonymous usage data* below). Kmito never sells data and shows no ads.

- **Internet access:** Kmito uses the internet only to send anonymous usage data after you opt in. The Google Play edition downloads no content from the internet itself.
- **Updates (Google Play edition):** When you press *Check* in Settings → About, and at most once a day when the app opens, Kmito asks the Google Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version exists. Google Play downloads and installs the update, as it does for any app from the store, under Google's own terms. Kmito sends nothing to Kelly Ideas for this.
- **Local-Only Storage:** All quiz attempts, earned minute balances, streaks, and user settings (such as theme and language preferences) are stored strictly locally on your device.
- **App usage (optional, usage access):** During setup, and when you choose *Set up again* in Settings, Kmito can look at how your phone was used in the last week, so it can suggest settings that fit. It asks first and opens Android's *Usage access* page only when you tap *Show my week*; you can say *Not now*. With your permission, Kmito reads from Android the time spent in each app, the hour of the day it was used, and how often the screen was unlocked. Kmito itself, the home screen and system screens are not counted. The result (minutes per day, your most used apps with their names, and your choices for them) is kept only on your phone, in a file that is not included in backups. It is never uploaded, never sent to Kelly Ideas or to anyone else, and never part of the anonymous usage data. Usage access is also used, on the phone only, to keep the apps you mark as *always free* free of charge and to count daily app limits. You can turn usage access off at any time in Android's settings; Kmito then works without these suggestions.
- **Microphone (optional, for voice answers):** Kmito asks for microphone access only if you want to answer quizzes by voice. You can decline and answer by tapping instead. When you use voice answers, Kmito passes your speech to Android's speech recognition, gets back text, and compares that text with the answer choices. Kmito does not record, save or upload audio, and it keeps no transcripts. On Android 12 and later, when your phone has on-device speech recognition, Kmito uses it and the audio stays on your phone. Otherwise Android uses your phone's default speech recognition service (often Google's), which may process the audio on its own servers under that provider's privacy policy. Kelly Ideas receives none of this audio or text.
- **Not Included in Backups:** The app database is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to your Google account or to a new phone. The same applies to your permission choices, the app's on-device usage log, today's snooze count, the lock timers, the internal lock token, the tamper alerts, pending Commitment mode changes and the family page settings.
- **On-Device Usage Log:** Kmito keeps a short log of app events (for example, which permissions you allowed or declined and from which screen) to improve the app. It holds no personal data and keeps only the newest 500 entries. It leaves your device only as described in *Anonymous usage data*.
- **Anonymous usage data (optional, off by default):** In Settings → Permissions you can turn on *Share anonymous usage data*. Only then does Kmito send app events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service run by Google for Kelly Ideas. The events say which screens and steps were used, for example which permission was allowed or declined, whether setup was finished, and whether the quiz lock is on, and which features you use, once a day each (for example "voice answer was used today"). They never contain your name, email, quiz answers, the names of your apps, your app usage or screen time, or what you do in other apps. Firebase also receives technical data that comes with every event: a random app-install ID made by Firebase, device model, Android version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from the network address (the address itself is not stored by Kelly Ideas). Kmito does not use the advertising ID or the Android ID, sets no user ID, and turns off ad personalization. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Google Analytics keeps event data for 2 months. Turn the switch off at any time: sending stops at once and the app-install ID is deleted from your phone. While Child Guard is on, nothing is sent, whatever the switch says. Google's own handling is described at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Data Deletion:** You can delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application. Anonymous usage data already sent holds no name or email, so it cannot be traced back to you; Google Analytics deletes it automatically after 2 months.
- **No Advertising:** Kmito contains no advertising SDKs and no crash-reporting SDKs. The only third-party service that receives data from Kmito is the optional Google Analytics for Firebase described above; the update check uses the Google Play Store app already on your phone.
- **This website:** The Kmito website (kelly-ideas.github.io/kmito) uses Google Analytics in a cookieless mode to count visits. It stores no cookies and nothing else on your device, so the site has no cookie banner. For each page view, Google receives the page address, the page you came from, your browser and device type, language, screen size and an approximate location (country or region) derived from the network address. It also receives outbound link clicks, scrolls and other page events. Kelly Ideas cannot tell visitors apart or identify you. Ads, Google signals and ad personalization are off. The app itself sends nothing to the website.
