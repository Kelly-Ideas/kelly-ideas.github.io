Privacy Policy for QuizLock

Effective Date: October 24, 2026  ·  Developer: Kelly Ideas  ·  Package ID: io.github.kellyideas.quizlock

QuizLock is an offline cognitive quiz and screen-time budget application developed by Kelly Ideas.

1. Data Collection and Sharing

QuizLock does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal information, user identity, or device identifiers.

  • No Internet Permission on Google Play: The Google Play release of QuizLock (io.github.kellyideas.quizlock) does not request android.permission.INTERNET and performs no network communication.
  • 100% On-Device Storage: All quiz answers, earned screen-time minute balances, daily streaks, and application settings (such as theme and language preferences) are stored strictly locally in your device's internal application database.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Ads: QuizLock contains no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or third-party telemetry libraries.

2. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can reset your data inside the app's Settings screen, clear the application storage in your Android system settings, or uninstall the app to permanently remove all locally stored records.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Kelly Ideas at kellyideas.dev@gmail.com.
